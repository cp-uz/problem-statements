# cp.uz problem statements

This repository is the PDF archive used by the [cp.uz](https://cp.uz) olympiad problem catalog.
Each problem has one stable, individually addressable PDF at:

```text
<season>/<event>/<set>/<problem>/statement.pdf
```

`manifest.json` records the source, language, provenance, SHA-256 digest, byte size, and page
count for every document. Official statements are preserved without content changes. When no
official individual PDF exists, cp.uz generates a PDF from its canonical Uzbek statement; those
entries are marked `generated`.

The olympiad organizers and authors retain ownership of official statements. Source URLs are
kept in the manifest for attribution and auditing. This repository does not apply the cp.uz code
license to third-party statement content.

## Historical international finals

The 2023–2025 IOI statements are available under the organizers' CC BY license;
see the [2023](https://ioi.te.lv/locations/ioi23/contest/),
[2024](https://ioi.te.lv/locations/ioi24/contest/), and
[2025](https://ioi.te.lv/locations/ioi25/contest/) official archives for authors,
translations, and license attribution. IOI 2025 PDFs are the unchanged
`uz_UZ.pdf` files from the official translation ZIPs.

EGOI 2024 and 2025 tasks are CC BY-SA, as recorded in the technical committees'
[2024](https://github.com/zehnsechs/egoi-2024-testdata) and
[2025](https://github.com/egoi-org/egoi-2025) task archives. APIO 2023 and 2024
organizer repositories are CC0; their official Uzbek translations are mirrored
from [APIO 2023](https://github.com/apio2023/apio2023_tasks) and
[APIO 2024](https://github.com/APIO-2024/apio2024_tasks).
APIO 2025 is not mirrored here because an explicit reuse license was not found;
cp.uz's season page links directly to the organizer's original statements.
Every exact mirrored source PDF is recorded in `manifest.json`; cp.uz does not
claim authorship of these statements.
